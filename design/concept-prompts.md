# Concept prompts (Codex gpt-image via knight-api)

## Shared frame
Generate an image: a polished product design presentation board, wide landscape 16:9, three iPhone screens side by side shown flat and straight-on (thin modern iPhone outline, no hands, no perspective), on a quiet neutral backdrop. App: "Say Less", a fully offline live voice translator for world travel. Two people face each other with the phone lying flat between them: each speaks their language and the other reads (and hears) it in theirs.
Screen 1 "Conversation": the screen is split for two people: the top half is rotated 180° so it reads correctly for the person across the table (Japanese), the bottom half is for the user (English). Show a real short exchange about finding a train to Kyoto.
Screen 2 "Language packs": choosing which languages are downloaded for offline use on this trip (size in MB, downloaded / downloading states).
Screen 3 "Onboarding moment": one frame from a creative first-run sequence (described per concept) where the user sets up their trip.
Controls are Apple iOS 26 "Liquid Glass": translucent refractive glass capsules and buttons floating above the content, with real lensing, soft specular edges, content visible through them. The content layer carries the identity described below. Restrained refined typography, tactile depth, real craft, legible text, invented content only (no real brands or logos).
Strictly avoid: purple-blue gradients, glowing orbs, generic audio waveforms, rows of flag emojis, sparkle or AI-star icons, neon glows, floating 3D blobs, generic dark dashboard look, chat bubbles that look like a messaging app.
